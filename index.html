About

Made for flags, icons, and pixel creativity

Pixel Flag Creator is a browser-based pixel art editor designed for creating flags, icons, and simple pixel artwork through an intuitive grid-based drawing system. Paint, fill, and customize your own mini masterpieces with smooth controls and instant export.
